About us

A small studio with warehouse know-how.

Krazy Koozies is Luis and Natalie’s custom koozie printing business. We print custom designs on foam and neoprene can koozies for weddings, craft breweries and taprooms, corporate gifts, holiday parties, and anyone who wants a can to look like it belongs at the party.

Luis spent years at a koozie printing warehouse in Houston. That’s where he learned the machines, the substrates, the packaging, and the shipping — the unglamorous details that make a finished order feel right.

Today, production happens in a home studio in Oakland’s Fruitvale neighborhood. It’s a small operation on purpose, close enough to talk through colors and files, and careful enough to pack an order the way a warehouse would.

What’s next

The plan is to grow from the home studio into a warehouse with two machines and a team. Until then, every order still gets the same hands-on path: a clear conversation, a test print, careful packaging, and a shipment that arrives ready for the event.
