Products

Foam and neoprene, printed with your design.

Two classic can koozie styles, both ready for custom artwork. Tell us the look you want — we’ll help you pick the substrate that fits the event.

Classic

Foam koozies

Lightweight, familiar, and easy to decorate. Foam koozies are the party favorite — a strong fit for big orders, welcome bags, and taproom giveaways.

Stitched

Neoprene koozies

A softer, stitched feel with a little more structure. Neoprene koozies are a great choice for brewery merch, wedding favors, and gifts people will actually keep.

How it works

Upload, test, pack, and ship.

A clear path from your design to koozies in hand. Natalie keeps the conversation going; Luis handles the print run.

1

Upload your design

Send a PDF, or tell us what you’re imagining. Natalie reviews files, colors, and details so everything is ready to print.

2

Test print

We pull a test so you can check color, placement, and finish before the full run begins.

3

Package

Each order is packed with the same care Luis learned in a koozie printing warehouse in Houston.

4

Ship

We send them out, ready for the wedding, the taproom, the office, or the party.
